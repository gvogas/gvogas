Term

TypeScript React Tailwind Cloudflare Workers D1 (SQLite) IndexedDB Workers AI Vitest

A student tracker for courses, timetables, deadlines, grades, and study time, used by a small group of classmates. It works offline and syncs when you're back online. 700+ commits so far.

Inside the build +
  • An "Up next" list ranks what to work on now and says why ("Exam in 4 days · worth 30%")
  • Grade math: weighted categories, drop-lowest rules, "what if" scores, and GPA by semester
  • Shared classes and team projects with roles, invites, an inbox, and an activity feed
  • AI import reads course outlines and timetable screenshots, then shows a preview before anything is saved
  • Private calendar feed for Google or Apple Calendar, push digests, undo, and JSON/CSV export

Architecture: Local-first. The UI reads from IndexedDB and syncs to a Worker API on D1. Pushing to main runs checks, applies migrations, and deploys through GitHub Actions.

Headroom

iOS watchOS Widgets Siri & Shortcuts Face ID

An iPhone app that answers one question: how much can I safely spend before payday? You enter your balance, payday, and bills, and it gives you a number per day. No account, no bank login, and nothing leaves the phone.

Inside the build +
  • Safe to spend = balance − bills due before payday − set-asides, divided by the days left, in exact cents
  • "Can I spend this?" check that recalculates with bills and savings protected
  • Home-screen widget, Apple Watch complications, and Siri logging with no setup
  • Face ID lock, hide-amounts mode, and passphrase-encrypted backups
  • English and French (Canada), with a one-time Pro upgrade and no tracking code

Smart Self-Checkout

Python Flask Raspberry Pi ESP32 MQTT RFID SQLite

A small self-checkout store on a Raspberry Pi. Customers scan items with RFID and pay with a membership card, and two fridges are monitored live by an ESP32. Built with Oliver D'Avino and Arend Markies.

Inside the build +
  • ESP32 firmware reads two DHT11 sensors and publishes temperature and humidity over MQTT
  • Live fridge dashboard; over-threshold readings send an email alert, and replying YES turns on the fan
  • RFID checkout, membership cards, receipts by email, and purchase history
  • Admin side for products, stock, and customers, with low-stock and restock emails

My part: ESP32 and sensor code, the fan driver, the fridge dashboard, email alerts, and the product, inventory, and checkout flows.
