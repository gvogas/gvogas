Scheduling App

Flutter Firebase Google Places API Dart Android iOS

A service company was running its whole schedule on paper, so they hired me to replace it. It has been in daily use since I handed it over.

  • Real-time appointment calendar with per-employee color coding and admin/employee role separation
  • Full client directory with accent-insensitive search across large record sets
  • In-app photo capture, auto-compression, and background upload to Firebase Storage
  • Invite-only employee onboarding with pre-whitelisted email registration
  • Light/dark mode, text scaling, and multi-language support, all persisted across sessions

Architecture: Feature-first folder structure; all DB access mediated through per-feature service classes, so screens never query Firestore directly.

AI Study Assistant

Hackathon project from MariHacks. Give it a topic, plus optionally your notes or slides, and it generates study material, with a coin economy and a quiz-linked plant companion layered on top.
